## 📌 About the Project
**Virasat Journeys** is an interactive digital heritage portal. It bridges the generational gap between youth and cultural history by transforming static textbook history into an engaging, multi-sensory digital experience. Combining glassmorphism aesthetics, Indian cultural motifs, and modern web architecture, Virasat offers children and students a vibrant playground to learn about historical monuments, regional folklore, authentic cuisine, traditional clothing, and music.

---

## ✨ Key Features
- **🗺️ Interactive Heritage Map & Directory:** Discover historical sites filtered by categories, eras, and geography.
- **📖 Layered Storytelling:** Age-tailored explanations (simple kid-friendly narratives + detailed historical context) paired with interactive timelines.
- **🎵 Regional Soundscapes & Voice Narration:** Integrated audio player showcasing traditional instruments/songs and multi-language voice narration (TTS).
- **🕹️ Gamified Knowledge Hub:** Quizzes, trivia challenges, and milestone badges that reward learning.
- **🖼️ 3D Virtual Tours:** Immersive 3D models and walkthroughs of iconic Indian architectural wonders.
- **📔 "My Memories" Scrapbook:** A zero-login, client-friendly memory journal allowing visitors to capture and preserve their travel visits and photos.
